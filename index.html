01 / Engineering

From genomic sequence to evidence.

The system combines reproducible data sampling, transformer fine-tuning, HPC execution, and an evaluation suite designed for scientific interpretation.

Core pipeline

GPN training engine

A complete PyTorch pipeline around the pretrained GPN backbone, including phastCons-quantile labelling, balanced genomic windows, reverse-complement augmentation, staged freezing, checkpointing, and metric tracking.

  • Hugging Face
  • pyBigWig
  • PyRanges

Adaptation

Fine-tuning workflow

A second training path for curated gene sets with reusable dataset construction, sequence classification, gradient accumulation, and experiment-specific outputs.

HPC orchestration
